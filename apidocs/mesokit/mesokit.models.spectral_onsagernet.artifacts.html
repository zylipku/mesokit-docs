mesokit.models.spectral_onsagernet.artifacts¶

Potential artifacts for OnsagerNet-family models.

PotentialLandscape is the concrete model-family artifact returned by SpectralOnsagerNet1d.artifact("potential"). It evaluates the learned potential on a constant-field slice, captures the per-mode Onsager multipliers, and, when the checkpoint loader attaches an Allen-Cahn reference dataset, builds the learned-vs-real potential scatter used by the tutorial notebook.
