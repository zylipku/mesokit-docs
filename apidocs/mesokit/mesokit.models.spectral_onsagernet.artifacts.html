mesokit.models.spectral_onsagernet.artifacts¶

Potential artifacts for the Spectral OnsagerNet family.

PotentialLandscape is the concrete model-family artifact returned by SpectralOnsagerNet.artifact("potential") and OnsagerNet.artifact("potential") in any dimension. It evaluates the learned potential on a constant-field slice, captures the per-mode Onsager multipliers Km / Kw on the model’s spectral grid when the operators are diagonal in Fourier space (always for the spectral family, only for circulant operators in the classical one), and, when the checkpoint loader attaches an Allen-Cahn reference dataset, builds the learned-vs-analytic potential scatter over real snapshots.

class mesokit.models.spectral_onsagernet.artifacts.PotentialLandscape¶

Learned Onsager potential, mode multipliers, and optional reference scatter.

Attributes: u_values, V_values: the constant-field slice V(u ≡ const). Km, Kw: mobility multipliers and the imaginary part of the reactive multipliers on the rfftn grid — shape (Nx//2+1,) in 1D, (Nx, Ny//2+1) in 2D, and so on, with a leading channel axis for a multi-channel model (one multiplier set per field). None when the model’s operator is not a Fourier multiplier (state-dependent M / W of the classical OnsagerNet). grid_shape, domain: the model’s periodic box. mode_numbers: mode index along the last spectral axis (the only axis in 1D), kept for line plots.

Render the artifact to matplotlib.

With a supplied axis, the most compact view is drawn there. Without an axis, a multi-panel figure is returned: the reference scatter (when present), the constant-field slice, and the Onsager multipliers — a line plot in 1D, an image of Km and of Im Kw in 2D and above (the k_z = 0 plane in 3D).
