mesokit.models.onsagernet.onsagernet¶

Classical OnsagerNet on a periodic grid, any dimension.

The field on the grid is flattened to a vector x ∈ R^d and evolved by the generalised Onsager principle

dx/dt = -(M(x) + W(x)) ∇V(x),

with a coercive scalar potential V and structured operators M (symmetric positive definite) and W (antisymmetric); see

mod:

.components. Unlike the Spectral OnsagerNet nothing is assumed about Fourier space: M and W may depend on the state, and the potential sees the whole vector at once. The price is that every network has the grid size as its input width, so the family is meant for modest grids.

∇V is obtained by autograd inside :meth:OnsagerNet.rhs, which is composed with the shared explicit steppers (Euler / RK4) from

mod:

mesokit.models.dynamics_base. The registered :class:OnsagerNet1d / :class:OnsagerNet2d / :class:OnsagerNet3d fix the number of axes.

Reference: Yu, Tian, E, Li, OnsagerNet: Learning stable and interpretable dynamics using a generalized Onsager principle, Phys. Rev. Fluids 6, 114402 (2021).

dx/dt = -(M(x) + W(x)) ∇V(x) on the flattened field.

Args: grid_shape: grid points per axis; the state has d = prod(grid_shape) entries. domain: box lengths per axis (defaults to 2π each). The dynamics do not use it; it fixes the cell volume for the potential artifact. potential: scalar potential module with forward(x) -> (B,); None builds a :class:~.components.CoercivePotential with three hidden layers of width 256. dissipation: module with apply(x, v) -> M(x) v; None builds a state-independent :class:~.components.CirculantDissipation. conservation: module with apply(x, v) -> W(x) v; None means W ≡ 0 (purely dissipative dynamics). step_method: "euler" or "rk4".

grad_potential(x: torch.Tensor) → torch.Tensor¶

∇V(x) for x of shape (B, d), differentiable w.r.t. the potential’s parameters.

Works under torch.no_grad() and torch.inference_mode() (Lightning’s validation loop): grad mode is re-enabled locally and the state is cloned out of inference mode. create_graph follows self.training so the training loss can back-propagate through ∇V.

rhs(u: torch.Tensor) → torch.Tensor¶
