# Tutorial

This tutorial is still under construction. The notebooks below are the
intended tutorial path, but their content and ordering may still change.

The tutorial lives as a series of Jupyter notebooks under
[`notebooks/`](https://github.com/MLDS-NUS/mesokit/tree/main/notebooks)
in the repository. They are rendered directly on GitHub — click any of the
links below.

The notebooks are the canonical source: they are the tutorial *and* the
integration test for the public API.

## Notebooks

- `01_introduction.ipynb` — mesoscopic-modelling motivation.
- `02_data_generation.ipynb` — Allen-Cahn data generation walkthrough plus
  a tour of `TrajectoryDataset`.
- `03_baselines.ipynb` — operator and symbolic baselines (FNO, DeepONet,
  SINDy) end-to-end on the same data, including each family's
  interpretability artifact.
- `04_physics_structured.ipynb` — Spectral OnsagerNet end-to-end, headline
  interpretability story (`PotentialLandscape`).
- `05_comparison.ipynb` — side-by-side comparison report driven by
  `mesokit.interpret.compare`.

Training examples use the public `mesokit.trainers` registry and the
`mesokit train` CLI. The CLI consumes registered HDF5 datasets, train configs
under `configs/train/<dyn>/<model>/`, and writes Lightning-free `model.pt`
checkpoints for later interpretation.
