# Fourier Neural Operator

Registered as `fno1d`, `fno2d`, `fno3d`. The structure-free baseline the
interpretable families are compared against.

## Model

`FNODynamics` wraps `neuralop.models.FNO` as a right-hand side,
$\partial_t u \approx \mathcal{G}_\theta(u)$, and steps it with explicit
Euler (default) or RK4. An FNO layer lifts the field to `hidden_channels`
channels, multiplies the lowest `n_modes` Fourier coefficients per axis by
learned complex weights, adds a pointwise linear path and a nonlinearity;
`n_layers` such layers are followed by a projection back to one channel.
`n_modes` is an integer or one entry per axis; `fno1d` / `fno2d` / `fno3d`
differ only in the number of spatial axes handed to neuralop. It is the
black-box baseline the interpretable families are compared against.

## The `spectrum` Artifact

Declared, not yet implemented: per-mode error and response diagnostics are
on the roadmap, and `artifact("spectrum")` raises `NotImplementedError`
until then. `mesokit interpret list` still shows the name. To read
something off a trained FNO today, build a post-hoc artifact from its
`rhs(u)` as described in [Custom Artifacts](../interpretability/custom_artifact.md).
