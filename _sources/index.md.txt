# mesokit

A toolkit for learning **mesoscopic dynamics** from data. The product
framing is **interpretability** — predictive accuracy is a precondition,
not the headline.

The user guide follows the stages of the toolkit. Start with the
[Quickstart](quickstart.md) for installation and the first end-to-end
run, then:

- [Data Processing](data_processing.md) — the trajectory-dataset contract,
  the shipped simulators, bringing your own files or your own equation.
- [Models](models.md) — what the shipped families compute (Spectral
  OnsagerNet, classical OnsagerNet, FNO), how to choose, and how to write
  your own.
- [Training](training.md) — what is fitted, the `mesokit train` recipes,
  the training views, the shipped `LitTrainer`, and custom trainers.
- [Interpretability](interpretability.md) — the artifact contract, the
  potential artifact and how to read it, comparing artifacts, and custom
  ones.

The [Command-Line Reference](cli.md) documents every `mesokit` command
with examples, and [Agent Skills](skills.md) explains the skills a coding
agent can use to do each stage for you.
