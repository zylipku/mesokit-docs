# mesokit

Learn mesoscopic dynamics from trajectory data, then inspect what the
model learned. `mesokit` brings together data generation and import,
model training, and diagnostics such as learned free energies, mobility
spectra, and prediction errors by spatial scale.

Start with [Installation](installation.md), then follow the
[Quickstart](quickstart.md) to generate a small dataset, train a model,
and plot its learned potential.
